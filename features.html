A practical tour

Tools that support the session without taking it over.

GenesisDM is a Windows desktop control panel for tabletop sessions. Its voice-driven suggestions are reviewable: a match is a suggestion, not an instruction to interrupt your game or change the scene without your say.

Local AI features

Narrative & scene matching

  • Optional microphone capture and local Whisper transcription.
  • Ollama-powered local LLM scene/entity extraction; Ollama is installed separately.
  • Searches tagged local art/music and shows confidence-scored suggestions for DM approval.
  • Manual text search/override remains available when you do not want voice suggestions.
Session tools

Combat, maps, clocks & mechanics

  • Combat tracking and the tactical map workspace with tokens, measurements, cover, and fog-of-war tools.
  • Segmented clocks and a flexible mechanics/house-rules tracker.
  • Supported system presets currently include D&D 5e (2024), Daggerheart, and Generic play.
Between-session tools

Campaign Crafter

  • Builds reviewable candidates for NPCs, locations, factions, creatures, and items.
  • Nothing becomes campaign canon automatically: confirm, edit, or discard each candidate.
  • Storyboard, scenes, threads, and run-sheet exports are useful but still early; expect rough edges.
Optional

Player Companion

  • Players on the same Wi-Fi can join in a browser using a code/QR and display name.
  • Remote players can use opt-in Cloudflare Tunnel (traffic routes through Cloudflare) or the self-hosted WireGuard alternative.
  • Remote networking is separate from local AI; it is not required for solo or same-network play.

Your notes, your vault

Bring your campaign notes into the session.

GenesisDM's implemented note-taking integration is Obsidian. Connect a vault folder to browse/search notes, follow wikilinks, and add session logs without switching away from the DM screen. GenesisDM does not provide vault synchronization; any sync from another service is a separate user choice.

Obsidian is optional. GenesisDM works without a vault. The vault path is selected by you, stored in your local app profile, and is not preconfigured in the trial build. No Obsidian plugin, cloud account, or running Obsidian process is required for vault access.

What works with Obsidian today

Browse your campaign vault

Search and browse Markdown notes, filter by campaign, follow [[wikilinks]], open a note in Obsidian, or copy its wikilink. Browsing is read-only; editing your existing notes remains in Obsidian.

Keep a session log

Add manual session-log entries from GenesisDM. Logs are written under the GenesisDM-owned folder in your vault; entries are not automatically generated from your voice.

Export GenesisDM-created material

Configured Obsidian destinations can receive Campaign Crafter exports, session recaps, and compiled character-sheet notes. Some generated notes are refreshed when their source changes; review the note's generated-content notice before editing inside those generated sections.

Read/write boundary

  • GenesisDM can read Markdown notes in the vault you selected so it can search and display them.
  • Writes are constrained to the configured app-owned folder, normally GenesisDM/; the vault write guard rejects paths that escape that folder.
  • The integration does not sync your vault to a GenesisDM server. If you separately sync the vault using another product, that product's own privacy and sync behavior applies.
  • Review generated-note behavior: session logs append; Campaign Crafter exports preserve content below their marker; compiled character sheets are app-generated and can be replaced when the sheet changes.
